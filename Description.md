Our project focuses on enhancing public safety through technology. 
The idea for this application originated from the growing concern that thousands of individuals worldwide experience harassment, kidnapping, or theft on a daily basis,
yet these issues often receive insufficient attention. Safe Route is designed to serve people of all ages, including women returning home after late work shifts,
students commuting to school, and individuals engaging in outdoor activities such as running, even during daytime hours.
The primary objective of this application is to reduce the risk of crime by promoting safer mobility. It achieves this by analyzing environmental and temporal
factors such as location, time of day, and historical incident data; to generate a safety score for each route, thereby guiding users toward safer paths.
The SafeRoute application was developed entirely using Python. As the project required a lightweight and easily accessible data storage method,
CSV (Comma-Separated Values) files were used to serve as the application’s database.
